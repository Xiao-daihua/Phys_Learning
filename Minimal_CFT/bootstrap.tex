
The core idea is of conformal bootstrap is to ask what kinds of
combination of the three data (central charge, Hilbert space/operator
content, structural constants) can give a self-consistent CFT. The
self-consistency conditions include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Unitary of the Hilbert Space
  \item Crossing Symmetry of the 4-Point Correlation Functions
  \item Modular Invariance of the Torus Partition Function
\end{itemize}
The crossing Symmetry will be discussed in another note extensively,
here we will focus on the Unitary Bound and Modular Invariance.

\section{Summary: BCFT as a Bootstrapped Theory}

I know, above notes are not rigorous. It's filled with arguments and
assumptions that are doubtful. Now, I want to discuss how should we
actually think of a BCFT. From my perspective, its messy to think of it as a ordinary QFT with
boundary, we analyze the boundary conditions and do canonical
quantization, find spectrums and correlation functions, etc.

Instead, just as CFT do, 
\begin{enumerate}
  \item we initially use the traditional QFT style
and tons of messy assumptions to find a possible structure or the
theory,
\item we take these structures as axioms and try to build
a consistent theory. 
\end{enumerate}
The second step is beyond the scope of this
note, but I think one should know there's this step to take. And if
time allows, I'll continue this adventure in another note.
